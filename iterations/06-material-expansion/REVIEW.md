# Material expansion review

The first composition sheet placed its footer over the notification silhouette. The second pass increases board height and moves the footer clear. Live semantic symbols now occupy the previously empty pockets and tiles. Dark copper joints remain structural; light is applied only along authored straight recessed rails. Curved decorative rims deliberately remain cold until a separately reviewed mask pass.

The cold images were inspected and copied unchanged. White seam/core PNG masks are separately editable and audited. SVG previews tint and blur these masks at render time; ember light is never written into the cold originals. Steady idle, disabled, focus and static reduced-motion snapshots are included. Hovered and pressed are illustrative light-intensity snapshots rather than native motion implementations.

The second sheet and its two screen compositions were inspected. The heavier steel is closer to the earlier artwork but consumes substantial space. Compact alternatives, real-size typography, long labels, RTL, accessibility scaling, exact display measurements and target profiling remain required. These eight total screen concepts across iterations 03 and 06 are not complete Android visual coverage.
