# Desktop review

Pass 1 established the cold chipped-steel masters and first independent light masks. The material reads much closer to the new reference than the earlier smooth symbols. The first mask canvas used 1280 units while the original artwork was 1254 px, causing a small alignment offset. The Wi-Fi orb also appeared too smooth.

Pass 2 uses the original 1254-unit canvas and adds irregular neutral hot spots, fissures and sparks in the separate core mask. The inspected icon sheet shows clean black channels when off, bright orange channels when on, and stronger localized light under active press. The Wi-Fi socket, Bluetooth diagonals and aircraft spine align with the original cold metal.

Pass 3 preserves the full cold export pixel buffer for off states, including RGB beneath transparent pixels. It also packages the 112 reused nine-patches, updates the explanatory caption to distinguish physical copper from ember emission, and retains the cold-off behavior on all four scalable frame families. The final control sheet was inspected at desktop size; its label wells remain quiet and readable.

The base master hashes are unchanged from generation. Six behavior tests pass; 450 technical conditions pass. Channel alpha coverage over material is 100% for all three icons. This is an alpha-overlap check, not proof of perfect artistic alignment at every density.

The frame geometry remains iteration 09 material; the icons are newly generated artwork. Large artwork is the current scope. Compact status readability, native mask blend parity, runtime memory/performance, live Inkscape round trip and target-device validation are pending. No independent reviewer or device pass has been performed.
