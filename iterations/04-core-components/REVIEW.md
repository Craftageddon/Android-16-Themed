# Iteration 04 review

This checkpoint expands the core families into 46 editable variants. Four paginated review sheets and the full component sheet were inspected. Text-field validation initially collided with its underline, and a context-item label/arrow sat too low; both were repositioned before packaging. Noninteractive progress, section, toast and tooltip specimens no longer receive a fake selection/focus marker. Disabled preview text now dims with its material.

The components cover ES-011..024 at prototype level, plus initial keyboard and controller caps. They are fixed-size art specimens; production resize/large-text/RTL behavior and native interaction remain unfinished. Frame faces are still smoother than the rich hero artwork; material harmonization remains part of the roadmap.

The source register verifies all 3,422 valid cached files by SHA-256 and records the one empty source separately. Its 1,272 normalized family groups and proposed source choices are rule-assisted candidates, not visual approval. Every one of the 103 roadmap rows has matching candidates or an explicit unresolved state. Related website/brand art, historical art, captures, audio and fonts remain separate roles.

Desktop checks cover SVG parsing, PNG decoding, alpha, neutral masks, absence of baked warm emission in cold bases, and palette contrast. Physical-device V3 remains pending. This is a WIP checkpoint, not final Android acceptance.
